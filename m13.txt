research: information fusion engine -- orthogonal fusion adds value, same-mechanism fusion subtracts

Principal pushed for a "Level 7" self-improving research architecture. Rejected the architecture
diagram as a build spec (zero proven alpha + ~130 hypotheses died today = capability outrunning
validation) and built the ONE layer with direct empirical support instead.

EVIDENCE THAT MOTIVATED IT: this session's only replicated result came from a COMBINED filter
(profitable + Sharpe + consistency + drawdown, 62% in-sample / 60% OOS) while every single
criterion alone gave nothing. The desk is full of weak singles never tested together.

scripts/fusion_engine.py -- discipline against the obvious failure mode (2^k subsets = false
positive factory): PRE-REGISTERED combinations only with a stated economic reason (no subset
sweep), Bonferroni across k, EQUAL-WEIGHT z-composites only (fitting weights on the same sample is
how a fusion engine becomes an overfitting engine), and every composite must beat its BEST
COMPONENT or it is relabelling rather than fusion.

RESULT -- 1/5 beat its best component, and it is the theoretically right one:
  flow_plus_regional (stablecoin_supply + kimchi)  IC +0.2687 vs best single 0.2102, lift +0.0585
  liquidity_expansion (stablecoin + defi_tvl)      lift -0.1067
  onchain_activity (dex_volume + protocol_fees)    lift -0.0107
  liquidity_plus_activity                          lift -0.0998
  full_stack (all four)                            lift -0.0562

PRINCIPLE EARNED: FUSE ACROSS MECHANISMS, NEVER WITHIN ONE. Combining two observations of the
SAME mechanism node adds noise (all three same-mechanism fusions had NEGATIVE lift); combining
genuinely orthogonal mechanisms (global dollar liquidity + KRW capital-control flow) is the only
one that gained. This also explains the elite-trader filter result -- profitability, consistency
and drawdown are orthogonal PROPERTIES, not restatements of one.

CAVEATS, not an edge: the winning composite inherits kimchi's SUSPECT-LOOKAHEAD flag, n=142
(kimchi binds), and most reads are SCREEN-UNDERPOWERED. Logged as a LEAD requiring the brain's
review of the ic_exceeds_contemporaneous rail plus more history.

Self-caught bug worth noting: first run returned INSUFFICIENT-DATA on everything because I passed
pre-z-scored inputs with zwin=1 -- a 1-period window has zero variance, so every signal collapsed
to zeros. The harness z-scores internally.
