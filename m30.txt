P0 FIX APPLIED: carry entry gate now FAILS CLOSED (_DEFAULT_RT_BPS 4.5 -> 39.5)

THE DEFECT: _rt_bps() returned the desk MEDIAN when a symbol had no measured cost. But
"unmeasured" is not a random subset -- a symbol is missing from the cost model BECAUSE it is too
illiquid to measure, i.e. it IS the expensive tail. Measured distribution across 30 symbols:
median 5.72, p75 21.30, p90 39.45, max 130.47 bps. The old default of 4.5 sat at the 43RD
PERCENTILE -- it assumed an unmeasured symbol was cheaper than 57% of measured ones.

WORSE, IT WAS DECORATIVE: at 4.5 with a 24h hold (3 periods) the gate required
funding > 4.5/3e4 = 0.00015, which is EXACTLY _MIN_FUNDING. The cost gate added literally nothing
beyond the pre-existing floor. It looked like a control and was not one.

FIX: _DEFAULT_RT_BPS = 39.5 (p90 of measured). Bar moves from 1.0x -> 8.8x the _MIN_FUNDING floor.
A symbol must now PROVE it is cheap (by being measured) before it can clear. This also performs
the majors migration by economics rather than by a hardcoded list.
BLAST RADIUS IS BOUNDED BY DESIGN: _entry_gate is applied to NEW OPENS ONLY and never to the
hold/target set (its own docstring), so raising the bar can only REFUSE opens -- it cannot
force-close or liquidate anything. Backup at /tmp/exec_backup.py. Parses clean, ruff green.

TWO OF MY OWN RECOMMENDATIONS CORRECTED BY READING THE CODE:
1. "Switch taker -> maker, halve the fees" -- WRONG, ALREADY DONE. _MAKER = True is the default
   ("maker-first execution, set via --no-maker"). I recommended a change that was already in
   place, which means fee-tier was never the cost story.
2. THE REAL COST HYPOTHESIS, and it is sharper: _mkt_or_limit does "MARKET first, LIMIT fallback"
   for CLOSE/HEDGE. So ENTRIES are maker-first but EXITS are MARKET-first. On thin books a market
   exit is exactly where slippage lives, and every carry exits. That is a far better explanation
   of the $876 residual than fee tier, and it is testable: attribute realised cost by open vs
   close leg in run_trade_forensics.
   Do NOT naively flip closes to maker-only -- the docstring explains why market-first exists
   there: a post-only close on a broken book may never fill, which previously stranded orphan
   legs (the -4131 PERCENT_PRICE path). The right fix is likely maker-with-timeout-then-market,
   not maker-only.
