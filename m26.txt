CME-vs-offshore basis: best structural candidate today (92bps) BUT likely a synchronisation artifact

TESTED THE BEST REMAINING HYPOTHESIS. Today's refined criterion for a survivable spread: width
>~50bps AND a HARD constraint AND free daily data. Pegs (1-15bps) and liquid-staking discounts
(10-30bps) failed on WIDTH because their constraints are SOFT. CME is the one candidate with a
LICENCE boundary rather than friction -- US regulated institutions legally CANNOT trade offshore
perps, the same class of constraint as Korean capital controls (the only class that has ever
survived on this desk).

CONSTRUCTION: cme_basis = CME front-month / Binance spot - 1; offshore_basis = Binance perp /
Binance spot - 1; SPREAD = cme - offshore. Both legs are BTC at the same date so direction cancels;
what remains is the price of being allowed to trade where you are allowed to trade.

  Mechanical verdict: HARVESTABLE-CANDIDATE (17x the USDC peg spread, ~65% of kimchi's 142bps)

WHY I DO NOT BELIEVE IT YET -- three red flags, all pointing at measurement, not market:
  1. HALF-LIFE 0.2d (~5h) on DAILY data means near-zero day-to-day autocorrelation. A real
     structural spread PERSISTS; measurement noise reverts instantly. This is evidence against.
  2. CME basis sd 92.4bps vs offshore sd 2.3bps -- 40x more volatile on the same underlying.
     That asymmetry is not economically plausible; it is the signature of one leg being noisy.
  3. Mechanical causes, all present: CME trades ~23/5 while Binance is 24/7 (Friday settle vs
     Sunday close), CME settles 16:00 CT while the Binance daily close is 00:00 UTC (a ~6h offset
     on EVERY observation), and Yahoo BTC=F is a ROLLING front-month so each roll injects a
     discontinuity that reads as spread volatility.
  Same failure class as the mSOL/SOL 539bps artifact caught this morning: non-synchronous legs.

STATUS: NOT a candidate yet, and NOT dead either -- unlike the pegs, the MECHANISM is real and the
constraint is genuinely hard. It needs timestamp-aligned legs (match CME settle to the Binance
price AT that settle time, not the daily close) and explicit roll handling. That is a real build,
and it is the highest-value spread work remaining.
