Turn stranded or unused datasets into executable MT5 cells. For each dataset, name the MT5 instrument it plausibly moves (directly, or indirectly through a proxy), the registered family that expresses it, and the publication lag that keeps it PIT. Prefer kind=recipe with exact params the family takes; use kind=hypothesis only when the family's defaults are the claim. Every row needs a concrete economic mechanism, a testable_claim, and a falsifier. No parameter sweeps: one row per BET.
