Find INDIRECT edges: lead-lag and proxy links between MT5 instruments and the economies behind them (commodity exporters and their currencies, rates and metals, risk proxies and havens, energy and CAD/NOK). Express each with a registered family such as relative_value, correlation_regime or carry. Prefer kind=recipe with exact params the family takes; use kind=hypothesis only when the family's defaults are the claim. Every row needs a concrete economic mechanism, a testable_claim, and a falsifier. No parameter sweeps: one row per BET.
