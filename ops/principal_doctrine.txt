  W = W(alpha, R, X, C, L, S): risk allocation, execution quality, costs, liquidity/capacity, survival. A 20% gain in execution or cost is worth exactly what a 20% better alpha is worth, and is usually cheaper and far more certain to obtain.
  INFORMATION VALUE:  DELTA_I is valuable iff E[log W | DELTA_I] - E[log W] > 0. A finding that DISPROVES is valuable -- it withdraws capital from a false edge and retires search space. The sign of the finding is irrelevant; only the shift in the objective counts.
