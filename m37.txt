WHAT IT ADDS OVER THE GRAVEYARD: a tombstone records WHAT died and a cause tag. An autopsy records
the FAILURE MODE and then AGGREGATES across families, which is where transferable knowledge lives.
negative_knowledge.py classifies PERMANENCE (can it revive?); this classifies AETIOLOGY (why did it
die?). 42 failures classified across 8 modes.

HEADLINE FINDING -- C_WRONG_TIMING 16/42 (38%), and DOMINANT IN FOUR FAMILIES (price-only/TA,
trader/behavioural, on-chain/flow, attention/social). The desk's most common failure is NOT bad
ideas or bad data. It is testing at the WRONG HORIZON, or measuring a COINCIDENT relationship as
though it were leading. That single number justifies the de-contamination gate and the horizon
search retroactively, and it says horizon/lead-lag design deserves more upfront budget than new
data sourcing.

not market findings. Exactly reproduced today: COOKIEUSDT flat slippage, mSOL 539bps, my NAV unit
error, the moat trade-vs-depth parser. Measurement defects are not an occasional embarrassment on
this desk; they are the second most common cause of death.

THIRD, AND THE MOST ACTIONABLE -- funding/positioning is the ONE FAMILY THAT IS ALIVE, and its
dominant failure mode is E_DATA_QUALITY (4) followed by G_TOO_EXPENSIVE (3). Its problems have been
MEASUREMENT and COST, never mechanism. That is precisely where today's work landed (entry gate
fail-open, 7.75x cost ratio) and it argues the remaining upside there is engineering, not research.
