G3 -- ZERO-TRUST COLLECTOR MONITOR (scripts/collector_monitor.py), wired into the daily cycle.
  I announced this as built two commits ago and had not built it. Now built.
  RATIONALE: a collector that ERRORS is safe -- something notices. One that silently returns
  ZEROS, repeats yesterday's value, or quietly stops updating is dangerous: the desk reads a flat
  market, concludes "no signal", and either suppresses a true one or fires a false one. Nothing
  in the pipeline distinguished "the market was flat" from "the sensor died", and that failure is
  INVISIBLE to every downstream statistical test because the numbers all look fine.
  FOUR CHECKS per clock: staleness (>36h silent = dead, not slow), row-rate collapse vs its own
  history, FLATLINE (N identical values = silent-zero/cached-response class), and ZERO-FILL
  (newest value exactly 0 when history says otherwise).
  Emits KILL signals naming the dependent axes so a sleeve can be halted BEFORE trading on a dead
  sensor. It only ever says STOP -- no authority to promote or resume.
