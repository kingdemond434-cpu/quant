PHASE A: mechanism graveyard + hypothesis portfolio + mechanism review board (one system)

Built as ONE module because they are one system: the mechanism taxonomy produces verdicts, the
verdicts drive portfolio correlation, and the review board enforces both at the gate.

1) MECHANISM-LEVEL GRAVEYARD -- the principal's key point, implemented.
   Concept archaeology blocks a PHRASE ("social attention momentum" impersonating "Twitter
   sentiment"). MECHANISM archaeology blocks a REASON -- including variants nobody has worded yet.
   8 economic mechanisms defined by "who is forced to act, and why can the edge persist?", NOT by
   dataset. Deaths mapped from the 42-row graveyard:
     M_PRICE_PATTERN        FAMILY KILL  price history alone, no participant story
     M_ATTENTION_DELAY  13  FAMILY KILL  information reaches us AFTER sophisticated participants
     M_SKILL_PERSISTENCE 7  FAMILY KILL  past performance predicts future performance
     M_FLOW_PRESSURE     6  FAMILY KILL  observable capital movement precedes its own impact
     M_FORCED_DELEVERAGE 10 ALIVE        forced unwind -- funding persistence IC +0.432
     M_STRUCTURAL_BARRIER 10 ALIVE       hard barrier -- kimchi/cny live clocks
     M_LIQUIDITY_WITHDRAWAL 7 UNTESTED   the moat frontier, needs book reconstruction
     M_FUNDAMENTAL_PROXY  3 WEAK
   FOUR FAMILY KILLS. Any future hypothesis mapping to one inherits the evidence and must show a
   NEW asymmetry or forced-flow story -- not merely a new dataset. This is what stops infinite
   reinvention: you cannot reword your way past a mechanism verdict.
   NOTE the two ALIVE mechanisms are exactly the two with a live forward clock, and the UNTESTED
   one is exactly the moat. The taxonomy reproduces the desk's real state without being told it.

2) HYPOTHESIS PORTFOLIO CONSTRUCTION -- ERV alone concentrates the book.
   Demonstrated on the principal's own slate: the top TWO by raw ERV (Market maker inventory
   stress, Liquidity fragility) BOTH map to M_LIQUIDITY_WITHDRAWAL. Ranking by ERV would fund the
   same bet twice. Correlation penalty halves value per prior selection in the same mechanism:
   the second liquidity idea drops 1.000 -> 0.500 and the portfolio diversifies by construction.
   With a larger queue this is the difference between five ideas and ONE BET WEARING FIVE HATS.

3) MECHANISM REVIEW BOARD -- pre-test gate, four mandatory questions: why should this exist / WHO
   is forced to trade / why is it not already arbitraged / what kills it. Plus automatic rejection
   if the hypothesis maps to a FAMILY KILL.
   Validated on three samples:
     "Low RSI predicts bounce"        REJECT -- 3 unanswered + M_PRICE_PATTERN family kill
     "Liquidation cascade exhaustion" PASS   -- names the forced party (liquidated accounts CANNOT
                                               choose timing), the constraint (book depth caps
                                               capacity), and a kill condition
     "Attention efficiency ratio"     REJECT -- 4 unanswered + M_ATTENTION_DELAY family kill
   JUSTIFIED BY MEASUREMENT: the autopsy found 64% of this desk's failures were TIMING (38%) or
   MEASUREMENT (26%), not alpha. A pre-test gate catches that class for free, before a slot is
   spent.
