# L0248

Naming what to watch is not the same as knowing where to look. frontier_intel.registry listed 22 organisations and graded their source KINDS ('official_site', 'careers_page') but held no addresses, and scout() matches intelligence rows that MENTION a tracked firm -- so with no firm page ever seeded, the only rows it could match were incidental mentions in material gathered for something else. Everything looked configured. Derive the crawl from the same table that decides who is watched, and report the firms with no address instead of letting them look covered.

## Evidence

22 firms registered, 0 firm pages in world_crawler SEEDS; after wiring, 21 seeds and 2 honestly-unseeded (Citadel Securities answers 403 to a non-browser client)
