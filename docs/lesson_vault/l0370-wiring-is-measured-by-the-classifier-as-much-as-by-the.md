# L0370

Wiring is measured by the CLASSIFIER as much as by the clock: an organ imported by an f-string (forest_runner builds research.countries.{code}.data_plane at runtime) or run by a SYSTEMD line the cron parser skips reads as unclocked, while an organ named only in a fence's own comment reads as wired. Derive the dynamic edges from the same source the runner uses, parse every clock declaration the manifest carries, and never name a path in prose that would make your comment its clock. The long tail that is too small for its own leg goes on a ROTATING battery with a published per-organ verdict age; anything left is retired to _retired/ with a reason, replacement and date.

## Evidence

2026-09-23 on VMI3500897: 200 executables with main() and no clock. 82 rostered on two hourly batteries, 85 retired with rows in docs/research/retirements.jsonl, 10 country data planes and japan/mandate.py reclaimed by dynamic_reach_roots, scripts/deep_mine_x.py and others by systemd_manifest_specs (85 units ops/crontab.manifest declares); final count 1 (ratchet 700 -> 1). The ratchet comment naming desks/mt5/scripts/arm_and_pass.py in full briefly made THAT COMMENT its clock -- the census read 0 until the path was unspelled. Registry build 216s -> 57s after deduping the reach frontier.
