# L0200

Never take an official 'Historical Data' CSV as the panel -- diff it against the per-period releases it summarises before building an axis on it.

## Evidence

Japan MoF FX intervention: the headline foreign_exchange_intervention_operations.csv holds 2 of the 4 daily events in 2024 (missing May 1, Jul 12) and 1 of the 3 in 2026 (missing May 4, May 6), while amounts on rows present match to the yen -- incompleteness, not a parse error, and nothing on the page discloses it. The quarterly HTML releases are authoritative.
