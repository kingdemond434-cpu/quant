# L0306

A ceiling derived from a measurement GRID is the edge of the sweep, not an economic limit -- and it will be reported as though it were one. If the curve is still rising at its last sampled point, the bound is the measurement, not the opportunity.

## Evidence

2026-09-12: heat capped at 22.5% with the growth curve monotone rising at all ten points (+0.00716/day at 2% to +0.02821 at 22.5%) and the free optimum wanting 40.7%. growth_curve got ONE set of bounds computed at HEAT_TARGET, summing to ~22.5%, so every grid point above was skipped. The reason string said 'sweep further out to earn more' on every pass.
