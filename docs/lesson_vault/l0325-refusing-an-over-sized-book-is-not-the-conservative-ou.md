# L0325

Refusing an over-sized book is NOT the conservative outcome. Scale it to the measured bar: every sleeve is then sized below what the optimiser asked, the total lands on the bar, and the relative weights survive. Discarding it falls back to a floor -- less heat on fewer bets inside the SAME envelope, which is a worse book chosen by an accident of arithmetic.

## Evidence

2026-09-14: the allocator solved 0.3000 against a measured survival envelope of 0.2250, so decision_core discarded the whole book and logged 'sizing: no allocator book' every pass. Every non-gold sleeve takes its size from that book; gold has its own floor path. The live state read 'armed=True pos=0 pending=4 brackets=[gold_asia] sleeves=45' -- 45 sleeves, 1 bracket. The principal reported the scalps and FX book had never traded, ever.
