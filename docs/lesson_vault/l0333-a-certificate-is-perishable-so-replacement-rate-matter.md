# L0333

A certificate is PERISHABLE, so replacement RATE matters more than roster size. Judge the research system by survivors minted per compute-hour and by how fast decayed sleeves are replaced -- never by how many sleeves are live, which counts inventory and not production.

## Evidence

2026-09-14: 65 live sleeves across 16 nominal families, but discovered(24) + session_range_breakout(15) + overnight_gap_decay(12) are 51 of the 65, and nine more are v2/v3/v4 variants of the same three gold windows. Measured n_effective is 5.592 against 65 sleeves. Roster size said 65; independence said about six. The decay organs (decay_monitor, drift_monitor, resurrection, revival_engine) all exist; libs/ops/scaling_laws.py fits survivors per compute hour, and that is the number to read.
