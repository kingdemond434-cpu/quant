# L0271

A derived artifact that COPIES a field from its source freezes that field at build time, so repairing the source leaves every consumer of the copy unchanged. `cost_surface` snapshots `pooled_median_spread_pts` out of the universe registry when it builds; `execution_cost` read the snapshot. Repairing 27 registry spreads therefore moved no ratio at all and the instrument went on pricing zero sleeves -- the repair looked applied and was inert. When a question is about what is true TODAY, read the live source and treat the snapshot as the fallback, not the other way round.

## Evidence

after --apply, n_measured stayed 0 until the denominator was switched to the live registry, then went to 12
