# L0356

An organ that publishes a verdict nobody consumes, or that is on no clock, is a defect even when its docstring is right: credit_assignment (F12, 'credit flows backwards from live outcomes') sat on no schedule for four days, read the live ledger and never credited a live row, and declared 'nothing here reweights a generator' while the closed-loop flag for delayed-truth credit stayed hard-coded False. The fix is the whole chain or nothing: schedule the organ, join the evidence it names, feed the two consumers (bandit worth, generator weights) by one bounded rule, and make the attestation read the consumer's basis field rather than a label.

## Evidence

2026-09-16: credit_assignment.py absent from hourly_cycle _costed names and box_tasks; _live_rows() read but never joined (use_live only relabelled forward evidence); check_closed_loop.research.delayed_truth_credit_live = False literal. Wired: leg credit_assignment (--apply), _credit_live via sleeves.json, bandit.realised_credit per arm, mutation_yield.realised_credit_by_generator, flag reads bandit.realised_credit.basis.
