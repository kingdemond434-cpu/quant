# L0181

Before spending a run's first effort on a convert-first directive, VERIFY the backlog is real: re-parse a sample card and compare against what the gate claims. A §33 tag on the line below its heading is invisible to parse_dispositions and inflates the directive.

## Evidence

mine_gate demanded 22 dispositions on 2026-08-26; 15 already carried a valid future deferral one line below the heading. Same tag parses to ('deferred','2026-09-05',2) on the heading line and to ('','') one line down. Moving 15 tags took 22 -> 7. R0514/R0617 family.
