# L0229

When a reader finds nothing, check the PRODUCER'S FORMAT before concluding the data is absent. Path-and-format mismatches fail silently in the direction that reads as a clean empty verdict, and they hide data the desk already pays to collect.

## Evidence

2026-08-29 BRAIN s9, edge_search.resolve_inputs: 4 of its 12 external families are dead and NOT ONE for want of data. swap globs *.json* over a dir holding 2026-08-27.parquet (1908 rows, swap_long/swap_short present); COT reads cot_tff.json when data/cot_tff/ is a DIRECTORY of per-currency parquet; macro scans top-level scalars of a doc whose top level is 4 containers; book reads data/tape/ticks/ which does not exist. Carry is the desk's only repeat survivor family and its primitive was absent from all 13,210,812 trials. R0717/R0718/R0720/R0721.
