# L0134

A reader that enumerates field names its producer does not write parses to ZERO and is indistinguishable from a healthy quiet organ. Build every loader fixture from the REAL on-disk row, sampled from the artifact, never from the field names the loader already understands -- a fixture made of covered fields cannot reveal what its loader is blind to. Applies to prompts as much as parsers: a producer contract that omits what the grader requires is the same defect wearing different clothes.

## Evidence

2026-08-12, three instances in one session: unlock_supply_series.py:299 omitted 'ts' (24,201/24,201 schedule rows dropped) and :395 omitted 'observed_utc' (78/78 supply rows dropped), both under a weekly cron exiting 0 -- fixed 601d4762. Same class in run_llm_trader.py: the brief asked a PASS for passed_on+pass_reason while decline_value.scoreable() required symbol+direction+probability, so 15 of 16 declines were ungradeable -- fixed 8275fd81.
