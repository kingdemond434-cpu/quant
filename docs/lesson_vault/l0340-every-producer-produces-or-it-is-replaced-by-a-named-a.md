# L0340

EVERY PRODUCER PRODUCES, OR IT IS REPLACED BY A NAMED ALTERNATIVE -- and DEAD, STARVED and UNMEASURED are three different problems that must never share a label. DEAD is zero output and the remedy is the declared inheritor, not another look. STARVED is real output far below its peers, which is almost always a budget or an input problem and is NEVER a reason to delete the organ. UNMEASURED means no artifact records the producer's output at all, which is a verdict and not a pass. Declare the replacement IN ADVANCE, while someone still understands the producer.

## Evidence

scripts/check_producer_yield.py, 84 producers over 7d on 2026-09-15: 53 PRODUCING, 12 STARVED, 8 DEAD, 11 UNMEASURED. alpha_evolution looked dead and was not: it runs, does not crash, and proposed 1 cell from 72 tests because it evolves against a book pf_allocation reports as ONE funded sleeve -- deleting it would have thrown away a working organ for being fed nothing. Separately the gate first reported asia_plane DEAD on the night it produced 513 cells, because the seat directory is data/intelligence/asia and the module is asia_plane: a name mismatch reads exactly like death.
