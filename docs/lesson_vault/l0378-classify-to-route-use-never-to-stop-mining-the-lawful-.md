# L0378

CLASSIFY TO ROUTE USE, NEVER TO STOP MINING. The lawful-ground pre-filter was a discovery brake the desk imposed on ITSELF, not a legal requirement: an ACCESS_UNCLEAR quarantine that kept the metadata and threw the claim away, a machine-extraction veto on PUBLIC_WITH_TERMS, a robots.txt Disallow read as a refusal, machine_use_allowed=false read as "registered, never scraped", an eight-domain clone allowlist, and a licence gate that refused to RUN public research code. Things published on the open internet are lawful to read. Licence, robots, source class and credibility are ROUTING AND PROVENANCE labels: they say what may be REDISTRIBUTED and how much WEIGHT the evidence carries, and they never stop discovery, ingestion, representation or testing. Keep the label on the row and delete the stop. Exactly five ACTS remain refused, and they are about acts rather than content: no credential theft or logging in as someone else; no bypassing an access control or a paywall; no material non-public information; no stolen or leaked private data; no personal data harvesting or doxxing. There is no sixth, and the ABSENCE of a rule is a permission, never a reason for caution.

ROUTING[ACCESS_UNCLEAR] moved from allowed_uses=(register,) at weight cap 0.0 to the full mining vocabulary at cap 1.0. sandbox.refuse_reason lost the host allowlist and both licence gates, which its own text called the single reason every seed is UNDISPOSED today. DatasetContract.may lost the machine_use_allowed veto that read UNMEASURED as a refusal. Measured while removing it: the hard-boundary marker scan never read the routers own claim key, so a credential dump arriving in the exact shape route() builds bypassed all five acts - widening the boundary reach was the one fix that went the other way.
