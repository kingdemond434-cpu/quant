# L0199

Report every per-account/per-entity normalised statistic with a trimmed or median twin BEFORE preregistering on it, and never read 'the effect grew with the sample' as evidence against an artifact -- the contaminating class usually scales with n too.

## Evidence

FX Blue 00:00 trade share: mean 6.11% -> 4.14% dropping 10 accounts -> 2.80% dropping 25 (uniform baseline 4.17%); median 1.90% is BELOW 01:00's 1.96%. 7 of the 10 accounts booking >=99% of trades in one hour book it at 00:00 (5 at exactly 100%) -- 00:00 is the missing-timestamp bucket. The prior run read 5.84%(n=107)->6.59%(n=525) as out-of-sample confirmation and preregistered H-20260828-006 on it; now refuted_at_premise.
