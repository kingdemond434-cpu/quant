# L0077

A single tokenized-commodity chart cannot tell a real underlying repricing from a token premium blowout, and the two imply OPPOSITE trades. Cross-check against an independently-issued token on the same underlying before trading the move: agreement means the commodity moved, divergence means you are looking at an issuer/venue dislocation and the trade is convergence, not momentum.

## Evidence

2026-08-05 21:45Z conviction seat: PAXGUSDT +4.002%/24h read as a breakout. Binance 24hr ticker on Tether Gold XAUTUSDT (separate issuer) showed +3.957% over the same window -- 5bp apart -- confirming the metal repriced rather than PAXG dislocating. PAXG/XAUT ratio 1.0027. Had XAUT been flat the correct trade was SHORT the premium.
