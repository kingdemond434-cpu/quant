# L0270

Do not score a channel your instrument cannot measure. E[log W] ~ (1/2) s_bar^2 k_eff has two factors: admitting a sleeve moves k_eff (breadth) and making held sleeves cheaper moves s_bar (quality). The first version of the breadth credit scored every research arm on the breadth channel alone, which correctly cut the arms that manufacture duplicates AND wrongly cut execution and exit work, whose growth is real and runs entirely through s_bar. UNMEASURED is a verdict, not a zero: give the unmeasured channel a NEUTRAL multiplier of 1.0 and name it as a gap that closes when execution.matched_fills is non-zero.

## Evidence

first pass: execution_improvement 12.16% -> 3.56%; after the two-channel correction 12.16% -> 8.06% with credit exactly 1.000
