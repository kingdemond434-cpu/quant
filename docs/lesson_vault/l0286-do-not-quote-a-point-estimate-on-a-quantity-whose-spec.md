# L0286

Do not quote a point estimate on a quantity whose specification uncertainty dwarfs its sampling error. Attribute the spread to its choices, publish the band, and name what would narrow it.

## Evidence

docs/BOOK_E8_MEASURED.md: three successive pass probabilities (92%, 67%, 73.6%) all inside a 49-point specification band; sampling band 13 points; the principal's complaint was correct.
