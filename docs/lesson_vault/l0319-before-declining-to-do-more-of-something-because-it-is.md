# L0319

Before declining to do more of something because it is 'expensive', MEASURE the price. A cost asserted from a plausible mechanism, repeated in commit messages and written into code as settled fact, will cap the desk forever and nobody will re-derive it.

## Evidence

2026-09-14: I capped session/chart expansion at 6, then 12, then 16 because 'each cell raises the deflated-Sharpe bar every other cell must clear'. The live report shows n_trials 597 and sr0 0.3786 IDENTICAL across all 4,235 judged cells -- a sealed campaign constant. The charge is also on the null-calibrated EFFECTIVE cell count, so correlated variants were never billed as independent. The principal challenged it; the measurement took one query.
