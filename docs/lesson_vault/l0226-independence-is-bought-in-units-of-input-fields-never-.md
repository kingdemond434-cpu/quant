# L0226

Independence is bought in units of INPUT FIELDS, never operators. Two alphas over the same field cannot be independent however different their transforms, so a search that is transform-rich and field-poor cannot produce independent survivors however large its trial count -- count your distinct raw fields before adding another operator.

## Evidence

CrisperX/50_WorldQuant_Alpha_Examples_for_Alphathon: 50 mutually-independent alphas = 50 distinct fields x 8 operators (rank+ts_mean cover 46/50), median formula 20 chars. The desk's edge_search builds ~4900 primitives per symbol from ONE field family and its external axis raises on every symbol (R0715).
