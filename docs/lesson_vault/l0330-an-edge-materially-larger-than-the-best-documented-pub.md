# L0330

An edge materially larger than the best-documented public benchmark, measured on a vastly smaller sample, carries a PRESUMPTION OF OVERFIT until forward evidence matures. Raise the suspicion before raising the ambition -- and flag it, never shrink the position for it, because the presumption is about the ESTIMATE and the remedy is more forward evidence, not less capital.

## Evidence

2026-09-14: the XAUUSD.asia certificate reads exp_R 0.5384 over 155 trades, 6.7 sigma. Medallion -- the best-documented fund in history -- is described by its own principals as winning on roughly 50.75% of trades. A per-trade edge orders of magnitude larger, on a sample orders of magnitude smaller, is the shape an overfit produces before it is an edge the desk has.
