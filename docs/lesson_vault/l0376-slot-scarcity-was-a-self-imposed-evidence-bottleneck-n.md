# L0376

SLOT SCARCITY WAS A SELF-IMPOSED EVIDENCE BOTTLENECK, NOT A RISK CONTROL. A forward clock gathers evidence and deploys NO capital -- shadow_forward replays a certified recipe against bars already held and writes an R series; promotion to LIVE is a separate, certificate-gated act. So every cap on ENROLMENT bought zero safety and cost only hypotheses the desk could never rule on. Rationing the cheap thing (clocks) to protect the expensive thing (capital) protects nothing, because the two are not the same resource. The honest cost of testing more is paid automatically and elsewhere: more clocks raise m, which makes EVERY clock's Holm/BH bar harder. Remove quotas on evidence gathering; never touch the multiplicity that prices it.

## Evidence

Measured 2026-09-23 on C:/opt/quant. forward_reconcile.family_budget declared decides=ENROLMENT_CAP_PER_FAMILY, an integer count of NEW enrolments per census family derived from holm_alpha(MAX_FORWARD_SLOTS=12,1) -- and NOTHING in the tree read it, so the desk carried the reputational cost of a quota with none of its arithmetic ever applied. The same ledger already records the cost of the pattern when it DID bite: 33 runnable certificates passing all ten gates and accruing no forward evidence, 'the whole promotion pipeline stalled behind a missing hourly call'. The quota now decides NOTHING__ENROLMENT_IS_UNCAPPED with gates_enrolment=False; the alpha arithmetic is still published as evidence. desks/mt5/reports/FORWARD_ENROLMENT.json after the change: 170 certificates, 170 enrolled, 0 missing, 0 overdue. scripts/check_forward_enrolment.py AST-scans the five-file enrolment path and fails the law gate on a quota constant, a roster slice or a len(enrolled)>=n gate; 17 tests in tests/scripts/test_check_forward_enrolment.py plant each of those and assert RED.
