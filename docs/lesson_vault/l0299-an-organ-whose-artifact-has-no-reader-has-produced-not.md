# L0299

An organ whose artifact has no READER has produced nothing, however correct it is. Before optimising a producer's yield, grep for who consumes its output.

## Evidence

2026-09-12: local_candidates.json had exactly two references in the repo -- local_converter.py which writes it, and organ_contract.py which checks its age. No organ opened it. Conversion yield was being argued about while every candidate went nowhere. Wiring it to the docket added 120 cells immediately.
