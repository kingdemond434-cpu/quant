# L0274

When you replace a winner-take-all choice with a blend, the losers do NOT get a small weight -- the ones the gate REFUSED must be excluded outright. Blending the dynamic allocator back in at 15% after the state proof denied it there grants, fractionally and quietly, exactly the authority the proof withheld. Separate the two questions: the certificate decides WHO MAY size (authority), the blend decides the MIXTURE among those admitted. Also: ruin is linear in h, so a convex mixture of non-ruinous books is non-ruinous exactly -- which is what makes blending safe at all, and is worth testing rather than assuming.

## Evidence

allocator_proof.select returned one name and discarded a score for every other contested book; MARGIN_FRAC supplied the softmax temperature so no new knob was introduced
