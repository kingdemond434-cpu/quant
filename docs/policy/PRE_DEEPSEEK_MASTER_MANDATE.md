For every extreme-return, persistently high-return, or otherwise economically interesting public
trader, copy trader, signal provider, bot, algorithm, strategy account, or strategy creator:
TRIGGER A DEEP LAWFUL OSINT / RESEARCH DOSSIER.

2. How much is explained by: genuine alpha, beta, leverage, position concentration, martingale /
