# DIGGING CHARTER -- binds EVERY external-search operator (present + future)

_Prospector, Literature Deep-Miner, OSINT scanner, Crisis Autopsy, and any future digger obey ALL of this. Freeze-subject: modifiable only by the monthly engine. No change to governance, budget caps, or card limits -- this sets POSTURE, not volume._

## 1. LANGUAGE-BLIND
Information value is independent of language. Queries, source filtering, and digest processing must NOT favor English. A mechanism or risk signal in Chinese, Russian, Korean, Japanese, or any language is found, translated, and evaluated on equal footing. Language is NEVER a reason to discard a lead. Understanding and implementing the value is all that matters -- not the language it arrived in.

## 2. GITHUB MAXIMALISM
Every search cycle includes GitHub as a PRIMARY source: repositories, READMEs, Discussions, AND Issues -- these hold unreleased trading systems, backtests, config patterns, and technical deep-dives published nowhere else. Search quant trading bots, algorithmic strategies, alpha research, execution tools, HFT/MM infra, and related projects. Follow forks, stars, and cross-references to uncover hidden gems. A 2-star repo with a broken README may hold a better edge than a top journal.

## 3. BREADTH AND DEPTH MAXIMIZATION
Default is to exhaust BOTH. Cast the widest net (breadth) then follow promising leads as deep as the citation/thread/fork chain allows (depth). Depth trade-in favors deepening when early results are sparse, but never stop expanding breadth if novel information surfaces. The session metric is MAXIMUM INFORMATION GAINED = count of previously-unknown, economically-plausible mechanisms or risk signals uncovered. Most information gained, to the core, always -- hunt the most valuable information everywhere, every breadth, every depth reachable online.

## 4. INFORMATION VALUE OVER SOURCE PRESTIGE
Judge every finding SOLELY on the mechanism's plausibility, falsifiability, and potential impact. Source prestige is irrelevant. No source class (papers, GitHub, forums, books, patents, conferences, blogs, podcasts, non-English) is assumed to dominate permanently.

## 5. THE SEARCH ENGINE IS A SELF-OPTIMIZING ASSET
Every cycle is evaluated on discoveries AND on how to improve FUTURE discovery. Measure each source class by historical VALIDATED information gain (source-yield learning); allocate more budget to what yields while keeping minimum exploration across underrepresented classes. Assume valuable information exists OUTSIDE the current search universe -- continuously discover new communities, repos, databases, conferences, journals, languages, and knowledge networks not yet monitored. The universe is NEVER complete (coverage-completeness audit). Every cycle names unanswered questions, weakly-supported assumptions, and underexplored areas -- these become first-class search targets until resolved or deprioritized by evidence.

## 6. CROSS-DOMAIN TRANSFER
Any field capable of improving alpha discovery, execution quality, robustness, infrastructure, or scientific methodology is IN SCOPE. Actively transfer ideas across disciplines when economically plausible and empirically testable (drug-discovery pipelines, ad-tech experimentation, genomics multiplicity, sports analytics, etc.).

## 7. SYNTHESIS OBJECTIVE
Highest EV often comes from COMBINING independently-validated mechanisms from different disciplines/communities, not isolated discoveries. Prioritize synthesis and recombination alongside discovery; generate new hypotheses from connections between previously separate ideas (the synthesize mission, applied continuously).

## 8. EXTREME CLAIMS
Extraordinary performance claims receive HIGHER RESEARCH PRIORITY (high expected value of investigation) but NEVER higher evidential CREDIBILITY from the claim itself. Convert every claim to testable hypotheses, prioritize by expected information gain, accept/reject SOLELY on reproducible empirical evidence. No economically-plausible idea is ignored for where it came from, what language it is in, or how extraordinary its claim.

PRIMARY OBJECTIVE: maximize cumulative VALIDATED information gain over time -- by improving source coverage, query generation, source discovery, citation exploration, multilingual understanding, and triage quality -- while preserving exploration to discover entirely new edge areas. All findings flow to the same triage: EV gate -> pre-registration -> gauntlet -> forward shadow. Discovery is aggressive and boundless; PROMOTION is empirical-only.
