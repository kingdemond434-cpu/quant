# CRISIS AUTOPSY LIBRARY — collapse forensics organ (spec pre-built 2026-07-18, per standing rule)

_Status: QUEUED under the execution lockdown — activates at S1 (when venue/counterparty risk
carries real money) OR immediately on an off-cycle event trigger (a major venue/protocol
failure happening now = run it on that event, logged). Spec pre-built so activation is
instant. Executed by the BRAIN with real web search. Obeys the DIGGING DOCTRINE._

## PURPOSE
Every crypto collapse left a paper trail of exactly the early-warning indicators and guard
gaps this desk needs: what insiders saw, when outsiders could have known, and which guard
would have saved a counterparty's clients. Mine collapses to their core; convert findings
into (a) venue-risk early-warning indicators the desk can monitor, (b) guard gaps → drill
scenarios, (c) counterparty-risk doctrine.

## TARGETS (one collapse per session, mined to DONE before the next)
FTX (the examiner reports are a curriculum), Celsius, 3AC, Mt.Gox, QuadrigaCX, Luna/Terra,
Voyager, BlockFi, Genesis, Alameda specifically as a TRADING-desk autopsy (what killed a
crypto-native quant desk from the inside), plus tradfi ancestors with mechanism relevance
(LTCM, Knight Capital -- the 45-minute ops death is THE drill scenario, Archegos, MF Global).

## SOURCE UNIVERSE (FBI-depth families; coverage per collapse logged to
docs/research/autopsy_coverage.md)
- COURT RECORD: bankruptcy filings, examiner reports, criminal trial transcripts, exhibits
  (chat logs entered into evidence are the highest-provenance leaked-chat source that exists).
- REGULATORY: SEC/CFTC complaints (mechanism goldmines -- they narrate the actual trades),
  consent orders, congressional testimony.
- EX-EMPLOYEE LAYER: post-collapse blogs and threads, interviews given after NDAs lapsed,
  Glassdoor-era warnings, deposition excerpts, whistleblower accounts -- rare, high-impact;
  provenance-grade hard (an ex-employee with documents beats ten anonymous threads).
- JOURNALISM: long-form investigations, books, podcast series; follow their citations to
  primaries -- journalism is the map, primaries are the territory.
- COMMUNITY FORENSICS: on-chain sleuths' threads, forum users who called it early (WHAT did
  they see and WHEN -- that timestamp is the early-warning indicator), archived warnings.
- ACADEMIC AUTOPSIES: post-mortem papers quantifying contagion and mechanism.

## PER-COLLAPSE OUTPUT → docs/research/autopsy_library.md (append, one dossier each)
1. TIMELINE: first knowable warning → collapse, with what was PUBLICLY visible at each step.
2. EARLY-WARNING INDICATORS: the 3-5 observables that fired first, mapped to signals this
   desk can actually monitor (withdrawal delays, basis blowouts, wallet flows, staff exits,
   yield promises, filing language).
3. GUARD GAP: the one control that would have saved clients/counterparties -- and whether
   this desk has its analog (cite the rail or the register row; if absent -> register).
4. DRILL SCENARIO: a runnable crisis scenario for the black-swan library.
5. MECHANISM NOTES: any tradeable mechanism exposed (forced flows, discount dynamics,
   claims markets) -- CLAIM-grade, to the EV gate like everything else.
A collapse is DONE when all five sections cite primary sources; DONE is auditable.

## RULES
Primary sources over narratives; provenance grading per the Prospector scale; no edgelording
-- the output is indicators, guards, and drills, not stories. Sunset: if 3 dossiers produce
zero new indicators/guards/drills, fold remaining targets into the OSINT lane at live.

## SECONDARY OUTPUT -- IMPROVEMENT FINDINGS (Digging Doctrine breadth, 2026-07-18)
Beyond mechanism cards, capture ANY non-alpha improvement found while digging -- structure, governance, data sources, LLM/prompt/process methods, research methodology, committee/panel design, execution, risk -- to docs/research/improvement_inbox.md, dug to its core with evidence, routed per the Digging Doctrine (structural/process -> improvement inbox + register; guard -> register + drills). Never discard an informative finding for not being a tradeable strategy. The same coverage-rotation + provenance + core-depth rules apply.
