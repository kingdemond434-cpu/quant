### The two distinctions that are the actual product

**ABSENT vs UNREADABLE.** The idiom replaced (`except (OSError, JSONDecodeError): return
default`) collapses them, and they demand **opposite** responses: ABSENT means no producer has
ever run (go schedule one); UNREADABLE means one ran and wrote garbage (go fix it, suspect a torn
write). A desk that cannot tell them apart debugs the wrong organ.

**Absence vs evaluation.** "Held at the floor because the evidence failed" and "held at the floor
because there is no evidence" are different claims, and only one was true.
