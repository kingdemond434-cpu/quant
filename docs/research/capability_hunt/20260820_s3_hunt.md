## What verification changed -- the proposal's own conclusion was wrong

Every *number* in Proposal A verified exactly. The venue's tick economics give EUR risked per
price unit per lot of **0.86 (BTCUSD), 86.41 (XAUUSD), 542.40 (every JPY cross), 86,414
(EURUSD)** against the constant's 92.00 -- CADJPY 5.90x and EURUSD 939.28x, matching A's "5.9x"
and "939x" to two decimals.

**But the mechanism claim was refuted, and it inverts the finding.** `sleeve_set()`
(`gateway.py:841`) **rewrites every promoted sleeve's `lot` field to `"auto_ramp"`**, discarding
whatever `promoter.py` wrote. So the literal `0.01` never reaches the venue,
`promoted_lot -> auto_lot` is **always** the path taken, and the defect was **live, in the ruin
direction** -- not latent, and not timidity.

Measured at the gateway's own EUR 1,683.89, with the desk's real recorded stops:

| sleeve | stop | lot sized | logged risk | TRUE risk | % equity | vs policy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CADJPY | 0.50 | 0.46 | EUR 21.16 (1.26%) | **EUR 124.75** | **7.41%** | 5.90x |
| USDJPY | 0.60 | 0.38 | EUR 20.98 (1.25%) | **EUR 123.67** | **7.34%** | 5.90x |
| EURJPY | 0.70 | 0.33 | EUR 21.25 (1.26%) | **EUR 125.29** | **7.44%** | 5.90x |

The correct CADJPY lot was **0.07, not 0.46**. And `cap_by_heat` compounded it: it charged every
sleeve **one** q -- gold's `realised_q(equity)` = 0.98% -- and computed `room = int(budget/q)`,
so it would admit **three** such sleeves for a believed **2.94%** book against a true **22.2%**.

The error ran **both ways**, which is why neither direction was ever caught. On gold -- the only
armed book -- 92.00 against a true 86.41 charged the desk **6.5% more risk than it was taking**,
so it sized 6.5% small. Nobody audits a number that is already small (L1.55).

**The conversion was never unknown, only never shared.** `research/book_sizing.py` names this
exact error in its docstring -- *"On the JPY crosses it returns yen and reads them as euros"* --
then implements the right one as a **local closure** (`:137`). `swap_exposure.py:111` and
`book_reality.py:257` re-implement it inline. Three correct copies in research, none importable,
and the money path kept the constant.

One alarm of mine was **refuted before it shipped**: I briefly read
`shadow_forward.per_symbol_costs`' `pts * tick_size * contract_size` as a 184x cost error on
JPY crosses. It is correct -- `engine.Costs` divides by `contract_size` and works in R-space, so
quote currency cancels. `universe.Instrument.spread_cost_per_lot` answers a *different* question
(account-currency ranking) and is right for that one. Two questions, similar names, both
correct.

## The one thing to carry forward

The generalisable lesson is not about lots. **Every fence on this desk asks whether a number was
fresh, present, agreed-upon, or produced by a gate that ran. None can ask whether it is in the
UNITS it claims** -- and a units error produces an artifact that passes all of those and opens a
real position. The next instance of this class will not look like a sizing bug; it will look
like a well-formed number.

Second: **an argument that a defect is "defused" by a downstream constant is a claim about
control flow, and control flow is checkable in one command.** A `.get`-style overwrite three
hundred lines away turned "latent, timidity" into "live, ruin". Read the writer *and* the
rewriter before concluding a path is dead.
