## What the first run KILLED — the proposal's own falsifier (b), fired

The capability was sold on a *"reachable yield band structurally forbidden to a tier-1 desk"* —
per-account promotional tiers and small pools only a small book can reach. **That premise is
false, measured on the desk's own 39,190-row `defi_lending.jsonl`:**

- Best stablecoin supply APY anywhere in the collected universe: **3.78%** (aave-v3 USDC, Ethereum,
  $162M TVL).
- Risk-free: **3.73%/yr**.
- Net of the desk's own mandated 300bps haircut: **0.78%** — the lending rung **loses to T-bills**.

So the floor is simply the risk-free rate, and the "band" adds nothing. **But the haircut is doing
100% of that work**: gross, lending wins by 5bps, and the **breakeven haircut is 5.5bps** against a
`DEFAULT_HAIRCUT_BPS = 300.0` that has **no derivation anywhere in the repo**. A verdict decided
55× over by an underived constant is an assumption wearing a measurement's clothes. The module
therefore publishes both rungs *and* the breakeven, always — a floor reading one input could never
notice the day the rungs cross.

**This did not kill the capability, only its yield-discovery half.** The meter and clamp register
stand: `hurdle_rate.py` is an ex-post scorecard of the *strategy's* return and cannot price a
clamp, cannot price idle dollars, and cannot distinguish losing to T-bills *while trading* from
*while flat*. The proposal was built with its weaker half labelled as refuted rather than shipped
as claimed.

**Falsifier (a) remains OPEN and is the single highest-value next question** — rowed, not guessed:
*where does the principal-signed $5,757.08 actually sit, and is it already earning at or above the
reachable floor there?* If yes, this meter is a constant zero and should be retired. It costs one
question to the principal. The fence prints it as its own `next_action`.
