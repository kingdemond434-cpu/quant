## Two defects this build's own first runs produced — both fixed, both now regression tests

1. **A fixed `MIN_BARS_PER_DAY = 20` silently dropped all 74 US share CFDs.** It is a 24-hour-FX
   threshold; a 6.5-hour cash session can never carry 20 bars. Their spread columns are 96%+
   non-zero and perfectly usable, and the artifact reported them as "no usable spread column" and
   read as successfully built — 123 symbols where 196 were available. This is the anti-hardcode
   law (LAWS §1) and WS-005 in one defect. Replaced by a per-symbol session length measured from
   the symbol's own tape (`max(mode, p90)` of bars/day — the median fails because on USDZAR and
   Accenture the splice is the *majority*), with no asset-class list anywhere. Exclusion evidence
   is now published per symbol as `splice_tickvol_ratio` (USDZAR 18.4×, EURZAR 14.8×, 3M 0.7×)
   so the exclusion is auditable rather than asserted.

2. **The fence's first version compared against the surface's *unconditional* hour cell and
   reported DISPERSED over a sleeve already measured at 2.5×.** A family does not trade every
   day: `overnight_gap_decay` fires only when the gap exceeds 0.75 ATR, and the book on exactly
   those mornings is wider. USDZAR hour 01 costs 1,496 pts across all days and 2,028 pts on the
   days this sleeve fills — so the unconditional cell reads 1.85×, under tolerance, silent.
   **Selection is part of the cost.** The fence now measures the actual fill bars and publishes
   the unconditional hour cell beside them so the selection effect stays visible.

Mutation-tested: restoring the fixed-20 threshold fails
`test_short_session_symbol_is_profiled_not_silently_dropped`; removing the `spread_pts` hour path
fails `test_costs_from_symbol_charges_the_hour_when_given_one`.
