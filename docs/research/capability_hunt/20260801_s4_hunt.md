### The safety boundary — the whole argument in one line

**It varies *HOW*, never *HOW MUCH*.** `excitation.py` has no vocabulary for size. Sizing,
leverage, the entry gate, the stop plan and every rail are untouched by construction. Closes are
**never** excited (`assign()` refuses the SELL side outright — a close is a certainty problem, not
a fee problem; incident #6 bought a short through zero into a +916,772 long). Worst case of a wait
arm is a fill seconds later at a marginally different price, bounded by a declared daily cap.

## What the first runs said — both fired, which is the success condition

**1. `check_excitation.py` → ABSORBING**, and it named the single best finding of the run:

```
ABSORBING: 1000CATUSDT n=5 bps=-74.6 -- no re-entry condition
ABSORBING: COOKIEUSDT  n=7 bps=-53.7 -- no re-entry condition
```

**Those are exactly the two incident-#6 symbols.** The executor's own comment (`:1238`) records
that its post-only close path — carrying neither `reduceOnly` nor a venue size cap — walked
COOKIEUSDT to +916,772 and 1000CATUSDT to +1,138,985. **The desk permanently excluded two symbols
because of its own bug**, fixed 2026-07-27, and *could never have discovered it*: the denylist
blocks new opens, and `n` only grows through opens, so `n` froze at 5 and 7 at the instant of the
block. The verdict was unfalsifiable by construction.

Both now carry a dated, bounded, named-change re-entry condition (3 probes, ≤$100, after
2026-08-15 — deliberately **after** the L1.38 launch window, since a probe is an improvement, not
a repair, and the first 20 live fills can never be re-randomised). The falsifier is recorded: if 3
clean probes still realise ≤ −20 bps the verdict is **re-confirmed on uncontaminated evidence**.
Note the confound runs both ways — `cost_model` puts COOKIEUSDT at 66.5 bps at $500, the most
expensive of all 30 measured symbols, so it may fail the ordinary cost gate long before the
denylist matters. That is a legitimate outcome, not a failed experiment.

**2. `run_cost_identification.py` → UNDERPOWERED**, and it refused to publish:

```
7 usable fills (< MIN_FILLS_FOR_RAMP=30) -- NO ramp evidence published
realised 0.228 bps vs modelled 0.176 bps -> cost_ratio 1.2955
identified: False | waits by event: close [1.373 .. 5.045]  open [14.375, 125.015, 247.021]
```

Two things worth reading twice:

- **The identification failure is now measured, not asserted.** Open waits and close waits have
  **zero overlap**. Maker wait is a deterministic function of side, so no regression on
  observational fills can ever separate "opens cost less because they wait" from "opens cost less
  because they are opens". No sample size fixes that — only intervention does.
- **The refusal is load-bearing and was proven end-to-end.** `--write-ramp` published nothing and
  left `data/ramp_state.json` uncreated. The underpowered `cost_ratio` of 1.2955 would have
  *failed* the 1.25 threshold anyway — but publishing a size-gate statistic from 7 fills would
  step the book up on fiction, which is strictly worse than leaving it pinned.

## Two bugs found and fixed *in this run*, both in my own instrument

L1.40: a found-unfixed bug is the conversion defect in its most expensive form.

1. **`unidentified_cells` crashed on first run** — it iterated `_`-prefixed metadata
   (`_measured_symbols`, `_measured_note`) as if they were design cells. The crash was the *benign*
   direction; the dangerous one would have been counting a prose note as an **observed** cell and
   reporting a design better identified than it is. Fixed with `real_cells`; regression test added.

2. **The TCA-epoch heuristic gave the exactly-wrong diagnosis.** "10 of 523 rows instrumented
   (1.9%)" reads as a catastrophic instrumentation hole. Measured per day:

   | 07-20 | 07-21 | 07-22 | 07-26 | 07-27 | 07-31 |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | 0/20 | 0/38 | **1/47** | 1/12 | 2/5 | **6/6** |

   My first rule took the *first instrumented row*, and that **single stray fill on 07-22** dragged
   the epoch back nine days and printed *"FIX INSTRUMENTATION — 60 fills carry no slippage"* about
   a recorder now running at **6/6 = 100%**. The true reading is **ACCRUE FILLS**: instrumentation
   is healthy, the fit is starved of *fills*, not of the *field*. Opposite next action. Fixed to
   use the longest trailing run of instrumented days; regression test added.

A third instance of the same class was caught in the fence: reporting *"go wire it"* at an
executor that **is** wired would send the next session to redo finished work. The fence now
separates `UNWIRED` (executor doesn't call the design) from `ACCRUE FILLS` (wired, book hasn't
traded) from `UNREADABLE`, and never guesses.

  **revenue** half of a carry desk has no realised-vs-predicted check while the cost half now has
  three.
- **R0251** (08-13) — shadow price of recorder slot 33 (a 2400 weight/min constant silently sets
