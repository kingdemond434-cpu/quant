## THE UNIT MEASUREMENT (why `swap_mode` is not optional)

Measured against real prices from `desks/mt5/data/universe/*_H1.parquet` and `point`/`contract_size`
from the registry, expressing `swap_long` as an implied annualised rate under both unit hypotheses:

| symbol | point | contract | swap_long | as PROFIT-CCY | as POINTS |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| XAUUSD | 0.01 | 100 | -65.67 | -5.11% | -5.11% |
| EURUSD | 1e-5 | 100,000 | -6.45 | -1.99% | -1.99% |
| CHFHUF | 1e-5 | 100,000 | -7774.0 | -7.28% | -7.28% |
| **USDJPY** | **0.001** | **100,000** | **6.35** | **0.014%** | **1.435%** |
| **GBPJPY** | **0.001** | **100,000** | **8.16** | **0.014%** | **1.357%** |

The two readings are **identical wherever `point x contract_size == 1.0`** -- which is every
5-digit major and XAUUSD, i.e. exactly the symbols a spot-check tries first. They differ by **100x**
on 3-digit JPY crosses. USD 4.3% vs JPY ~0.75% is a ~3.5% gross differential; +1.435% net of broker
markup is economically coherent and +0.014% is not, so **points is the correct reading here** -- but
that is an INFERENCE from plausibility, not the broker's declaration, and it is not recorded as one.
`libs/costs/mt5_calibration.py:92` already implements the conversion (`swap * point * contract_size`
when `swap_mode == 0`); it just never had a `swap_mode` to read.

**Consequence: the desk's only financing history is uninterpretable.** The five archived snapshots
(`desks/mt5/data/intelligence/broker_swaps/discoveries_20260826_{0622..1022}.json`, 244 symbols
each, tracked in git) carry the value and not the unit. **The desk has recorded the financing number
five times and its unit zero times.** They were therefore NOT backfilled into
`contract_terms/`: handing the carry family numbers whose scale is unknown is worse than handing it
nothing, and the honest repair is to run the real recorder.
