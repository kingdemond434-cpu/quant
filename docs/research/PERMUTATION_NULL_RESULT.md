# Permutation null on real perps — first measured result

Generated by `scripts/measure_permutation_null.py`; raw artifact `reports/permutation_null.json`
(gitignored, so the numbers are transcribed here). Method: `libs/validation/bar_permutation.py`,
1,000 permutations per asset, OKX confirmed daily bars, returns net of the desk's 3bp
per-turnover cost assumption (`generators.net_returns`).

## The instrument was certified before the results were read

| asset | bars | control (buy-and-hold) p | cc variance ratio | lag-1 autocorr |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| BTC | 2,438 | 1.0000 | 1.0000 | −0.002 → −0.0021 |
| ETH | 2,436 | 1.0000 | 1.0000 | +0.020 → −0.0060 |
| SOL | 2,017 | 1.0000 | 1.0000 | −0.029 → +0.0028 |

Buy-and-hold has no timing skill by construction and the permutation reorders returns without
changing them, so `p = 1.0000` is the correct answer and confirms the null is unbiased. This
check is not decoration: the source's own construction (two independent shuffles) scored 0.007
here, and a strict `>=` comparison scored 0.035 on float dust. Both were caught by this control.

## Multiplicity, priced

48 tests (24 pre-declared rules × 3 assets):

| threshold | observed | expected under null | excess |
|---|---|---|---|
| p < 0.10 | 17 | 4.8 | 3.5× |
| p < 0.05 | 10 | 2.4 | 4.2× |
| p < 0.01 | 3 | 0.5 | 6.0× |

Benjamini-Hochberg at FDR 10% keeps **2**. Holm at FWER 5% keeps **1**.

**Both corrections understate here, and the reason is measured rather than argued.** They assume
independent tests. These are not: `reports/live_book_concentration.json` puts mean pairwise
correlation across these perps at **0.682**, so three assets are closer to ~1.5 independent
confirmations than 3 — and `ma_cross[10,30]` versus `ma_cross[20,50]` are nearly the same rule.
This is the same over-correction the 2026-08-01 gate audit found. The raw excess and the
corrected count **bracket** the truth; neither is it.

The rules were **pre-declared** in `libs/autodiscovery/generators.py`, not searched. This is not a
heat map's best cell.

## Mean reversion goes the other way

`zscore_fade` and `shock_fade` return **negative** annualised Sharpe (−0.52 on BTC) at p ≈ 0.86.
That is the desk-internal evidence behind lesson L0054, and it agrees with two external studies
that share no machinery with this one (Algovibes' 350,000-backtest BTC sweep found zero
mean-reversion walk-forward survivors; IQ Capital's 10,000-trader study ranks reversal last).

## Why the old gate found none of this

These land at annualised Sharpe 0.8–1.2 — inside the 0.5–1.5 band where an external
131,441-backtest sweep observes genuine verified edge to live. `MIN_ADMISSION_OOS_SHARPE` was
shipped in per-bar units and required **4.78 annualised**.

## What this is NOT

- **Not promoted, and not a promotion argument.** Admission grants a forward clock, never capital,
  and nothing here touches the promotion bar.
- **No walk-forward split.** The permutation prices whether the *order* of bars is informative. It
  does not price whether you would have selected this rule in advance. Those are different
  questions and only the first is answered.
- **One venue, daily bars, three correlated assets.** The correlation is the binding limit on how
  much the cross-asset agreement is worth.
- **In-sample over the full history.** Every bar was used.
