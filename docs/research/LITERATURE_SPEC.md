# LITERATURE DEEP-MINER — the second external-information organ (2026-07-18)

_PRINCIPAL'S ONE RULE, verbatim spirit: the more information gained, to the core, the better —
always. A paper skimmed is noise; a paper mined to its mechanism is an asset._

_Executed by the BRAIN monthly (cadence key `last_lit_deepdive`; the daily collector
`collect_research_feed.py` keeps feeding the inbox — this duty is the DEEP pass over it plus
active hunting). Complements the Prospector: the Prospector mines practitioners and records;
this mines FORMAL RESEARCH. Zero deployment authority: output enters only via EV gate →
pre-registration → gauntlet._

## MISSION (per session: ≤15 searches/fetches, depth over breadth)
1. INBOX FIRST: triage every unread paper in the research feed inbox. For each: extract the
   MECHANISM in one paragraph (who loses money and why they persist), or discard with a
   one-line reason. Never summarize — summaries are the failure mode.
2. CITATION-CHAIN DIG: for any promising paper, follow citations BACKWARD (its sources) and
   FORWARD (who cited it) at least 2 levels. The alpha is rarely in the famous paper; it is
   in the overlooked reference or the failed replication.
3. REPLICATION SCAN: search for published replications/refutations of any candidate mechanism
   before it advances. A mechanism that failed replication enters the graveyard as prior, not
   the queue.
4. ADAPT: map each surviving mechanism to crypto-perp data the desk HAS (funding, OI/LS,
   liquidation stream, FRED macro, recorder microstructure) — or name exactly what data it
   would need. No mapping, no card.

## SOURCE UNIVERSE + COVERAGE (log to docs/research/literature_coverage.md; >=40% of budget
to least-recently-covered families)
- arXiv q-fin (all subcategories, not just the feed's slice) + stat.ML for method papers.
- SSRN: market microstructure, asset pricing anomalies, crypto/DeFi working papers.
- PRACTITIONER RESEARCH (published, free, criminally under-mined): AQR Insights, Man
  Institute, Two Sigma papers, BIS/IMF/Fed notes on crypto market structure.
- JOURNALS (free/preprint versions): Journal of Finance/JFE/RFS anomaly literature,
  Journal of Portfolio Management practitioner pieces.
- THESES + DISSERTATIONS: university repositories — detailed, honest, nobody reads them.
- FAILED-REPLICATION literature: the replication-crisis papers ARE a map of what not to test.
- NON-ENGLISH academic: Chinese/Japanese/Korean finance journals via abstracts + translation.

## OUTPUT (per session)
- ≤3 mechanism cards (same 8-field format as the Prospector, provenance = paper + replication
  status), appended to docs/research/literature_cards.md.
- Updated coverage table + a one-line "state of the literature" note: which research area
  moved this month.
- ZERO cards is valid and creditable — the literature is mostly mined-out beta; saying so
  honestly beats manufacturing candidates.

## RULES
Published ≠ true (most anomalies die post-publication — cite McLean-Pontiff as standing
prior); in-sample tables are CLAIM-grade; the desk's own gauntlet is the only arbiter.
Graveyard cross-check mandatory. Every test spends DSR budget — cards compete through the EV
gate like everything else. Sunset: zero accepted cards in 2 quarters → fold back into the
passive daily feed.
