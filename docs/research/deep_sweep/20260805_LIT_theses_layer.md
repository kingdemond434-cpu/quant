## THE THESIS OF THIS GROUND, stated so it can be tested rather than assumed
A thesis is the only literature genre **contractually obliged to report what failed**. A journal
paper hides its dead specifications behind a publication filter; a thesis has a *chapter* for them,
plus a "further work" section that is a free option on untested alphas, plus a data chapter that
names exact feeds, endpoints and vintages. That makes a thesis simultaneously **free graveyard
material** (a documented negative the desk adopts without spending a multiplicity slot) and **free
hypotheses**. Nobody reads them. That is the claimed edge.
**Test of the thesis, answered in the closing section, not assumed here.**
