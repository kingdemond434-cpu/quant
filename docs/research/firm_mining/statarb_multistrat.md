## Caveats and notes
- **Unreachable, recorded so it is not mined again:** SIP-vs-direct-feed latency arbitrage (SEC Citadel Securities order 2017), Citadel/Teza high-frequency trading, triangular arbitrage at tick speed, and XTX/HRT/Jump/Virtu/Tower market making.
- **The 151 Trading Strategies book edition was retracted by Springer.** The SSRN paper remains, so rows citing it are tagged REPORTED rather than DOCUMENTED.
- **Muller (PDT) says "Depth is more important than breadth"** and would rather have one Sharpe-2 strategy than five Sharpe-1 strategies. That is a direct counterpoint to the E8 plan of counting independent mechanisms, and is worth weighing.
- **Deep Momentum's "1:20 PM gamma trap" story is SPECULATED** (anonymous sourcing), so no row relies on it.
- **The Jane Street v. Millennium answer gives no mechanism details.** It only confirms that the India options profits were significant.
