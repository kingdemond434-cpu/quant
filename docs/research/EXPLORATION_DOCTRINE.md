# EXPLORATION DOCTRINE — permanent, non-negotiable (principal 2026-07-27)

## The mandate

> Always increase diversification while maintaining full dedication to each at maximum depth.
> Always branch out. Self-improving. Never an exhausted ceiling. The mission of new information
> sources is permanent, no matter what.

Binding on every miner, digger, prospector, cycle and allocator. Freeze-subject.

## The four laws

**L1 — MONOTONIC BRANCH COUNT.** The number of tracked information branches never decreases.
A branch may be *down-weighted* on evidence; it is never *deleted*. Archive-never-delete, exactly
as the memory doctrine already requires. Rationale: a branch at zero attention can never generate
the evidence that would revive it — zero is an absorbing state, and absorbing states are how a
research programme quietly dies.

**L2 — DEPTH IS A CADENCE GUARANTEE, NOT A BUDGET SHARE.** "Full dedication to each" cannot mean
an equal slice of a finite pie — with N growing, equal slices go to zero and depth collapses into
skimming. So depth is expressed as a **guaranteed revisit cadence**: every branch is re-dug within
its cadence window, to exhaustion criteria, regardless of how small its current weight is. Weight
governs *frequency and intensity*; cadence governs *never being abandoned*. This is what makes
"diversify a lot" and "max depth on each" compatible instead of contradictory.

**L3 — PERMANENT NEW-BRANCH SLICE, RATCHETED.** A non-negotiable share of exploration goes to
information classes that DO NOT EXIST YET. It never decays, because unexplored classes have no
track record to decay from. It **ratchets upward** as existing branches saturate: the more of the
known universe that is observed, the more budget flows to the unknown. Saturation is a signal to
expand, never a signal to stop. (Budget expression of DIGGING_CHARTER §12.)

**L4 — THE ONE THING THAT NEVER SCALES: CONFIRMATION.** Generation, sourcing and Stage-A screening
are UNLIMITED and carry ZERO promotion authority. The Stage-B forward-confirmation budget is FIXED
and must NEVER scale with discovery volume. At α=0.05, 10,000 screens yield ~500 false positives;
only a fixed, Holm-corrected confirmation budget converts unlimited search into real knowledge
instead of industrial p-hacking. **This law is what makes L1–L3 safe.** Anyone proposing to raise
confirmation slots "because we now generate more" has inverted the entire logic.

## Ceiling clause

There is no exhausted state. "Everything is covered" is always a false report — it means the map
is stale, not the territory finite. Saturation of a mechanism (e.g. M1 liquidity at 7/7 observed
nodes) retires *new sensors on that chain*, and simultaneously **raises** the new-branch slice.
Coverage completeness is re-derived blind each quarter and diffed against the map; anything the
blind re-derivation names that the map lacks enters rotation at status `never-visited`.

## What "self-improving" means operationally

Not "the desk gets bigger." The desk gets **better at knowing what is true**:
- every refutation is PAID in the allocator (closing a family prevents all future waste),
- every methodological rail earned from a near-miss becomes permanent and non-skippable
  (gapped-window, cohort-perturbation stability, power reporting, horizon-adjacency,
  SUSPECT-LOOKAHEAD — all earned from real errors caught in-flight, 2026-07-23..27),
- the allocator re-weights from realised information gain, never from decree.

A desk that only measured "new sources added" would optimise for hoarding. The metric is
**validated discoveries per unit of confirmation budget** — breadth is the input, not the score.
