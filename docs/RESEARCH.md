# RESEARCH — the one research mandate (every miner, hunter, digger, generator, screen, test, desk)

**Status: OPERATIVE. Companion to `docs/LAWS.md` (which governs on conflict; the sealed core
governs both).** This document merges every research mandate the desk has ever issued — the
Tier-1 controller mandate, the elite-intelligence mandate (all three parts), the digging charter,
the promotion protocol, the operating/measurement/exploration doctrines, the two-stage discovery
law, the net-compounding mandate and the research duties of the principal doctrine — compacted
without retirement: annexes remain verbatim under their banners, reachable via
`scripts/vault_search.py`. **Every research organ reads this file at cycle start; it is the
first standing order in every dig prompt.**

**THE INTERPRETATION RULE (LAWS §0) GOVERNS THIS FILE: every section is a weapon for finding
edges and compounding capital, never a compliance exercise. A research cycle is judged by the
alpha, survivors and validated information it produced — not by the duties it recited. Where a
duty and a hunt compete for the same budget, the hunt wins unless the duty protects evidence
integrity or survival. The research system never mistakes tending its own mandate for research.**

**The posture is maximum aggression under the rails: E[log W_T] is the only end; the discovery
rate is its research-end reading; both are scored every cycle. Breadth and depth are never
throttled — conversion scales UP to meet discovery, never the reverse. A hypothesis never
generated is lost permanently. Surplus discovery is inventory. An empty forward slot is idle
capital's research twin. Timidity in search — narrow queries, English-only ground, shallow
passes, quota-shaped stopping, "exhausted" without per-axis evidence — is the same scored defect
as idle capital (LAWS §2a).**

## 1. UNIVERSE AND SCOPE

The hunted universe is the **full MT5/Fusion universe** (LAWS §1): FX majors/crosses/exotics,
XAUUSD/XAGUSD and metals, equity indices, energy, softs, US share CFDs, Fusion-executable crypto
CFDs. Crypto-exchange-native ground is NEVER hunted; crypto reference data only where it
measurably informs an MT5 instrument. Session structure (Asia/London/NY), rollover/swap
mechanics, contract specs and broker microstructure are first-class search dimensions — the MT5
analogue of the funding/liquidation mechanics the old universe hunted.

**Anti-hardcode (LAWS §1):** universes come from registries (`desks/mt5/universe/universe.json`,
the source registry); every literal list in a miner is a bootstrap seed and must say so. A
hardcoded channel/symbol/account list that caps exploration is a defect of the WS-005 class.

## 2. THE CANONICAL LOOP AND WHAT TO HUNT

Unknown → information advantage → hypothesis → validation → deployable alpha → compounded capital
(L1.10). The desk converges on Tier-1 capability permanently (Tier-1 convergence objective):
every cycle runs the brutal capability-gap attack — name the largest gap between this desk and a
Tier-1 desk, attack the highest gap first, log what closed.

Hunt, permanently and in parallel: **unknown-unknowns** (reserve capacity for unestimable
exploration; question whether the search space itself is incomplete); **survivor white-space**
(the map of where survivors exist and where nothing has ever been tried); **discovery-frontier
estimation** (how much remains, estimated, never assumed); **asymmetric-information archaeology**
(L1.11a — eras, dead platforms, non-English ecosystems, poorly-indexed ground, ranked by
reverse-engineering cost per unit effort); **what is too small for funds** (§42 — micro-lot
viable edges, exotic crosses, session-boundary effects, small-capacity ground where small IS the
advantage); **cold exploration** (master 40 — ground with no prior, entered deliberately);
**crowding and edge diffusion** (master 68–69 — half-life measured, replacements hunted BEFORE
decay, L1.19). No permanent hypothesis exhaustion (master 39): "exhausted" requires per-axis
evidence (L1.51) and is always reversible.

## 3. SOURCES AND SEARCH DISCIPLINE (the digging charter, carried whole)

- **Language-blind, always.** EN, CN, RU, JP, KR, AR, PT, TR, ES are one ground. Native operators
  via the Search Operator Library (`docs/research/search_operator_library.md`) — the single
  source of truth for ALL search capability; every new operator lands there with provenance.
- **GitHub maximalism**; archives, Wayback/CDX, academic mirrors, government portals, vendor free
  tiers, forgotten APIs, community lakes. FREE-FIRST in every language; a paid path only after
  the documented free hunt fails, graded residual_gap in the universe map.
- **Information value over source prestige.** The comment/reply layer is first-class. Extreme
  claims are mined for their mechanism and screened like everything else — social proof is NEVER
  evidence, in any language, from any source.
- **§13 LEGITIMACY GATE, absolute in every language:** a licence forbidding the use is a HARD
  STOP; the access boundary is a hard limit, not a preference. No credential walls, no scraping
  behind auth that terms forbid, no gray-area "it's public enough".
- **The search engine is a self-optimizing asset:** operators, lexicons and diaspora expand every
  dig; digger parity — every upgrade propagates to ALL diggers (no digger left behind);
  search-space expansion is a standing rule; canary searches and the information-ecology monitor
  watch for ground shifting under us; discovery counterfactuals ("what would have found this
  sooner?") feed the operator library.
- **Cross-domain transfer:** a mechanism found on any ground is tested for MT5 transfer; the
  structure transfers even when the wrapper does not.
- **New data sources are a logged per-dig deliverable**, screened in the SAME run
  (screen-on-discovery duty): discovering a dataset is half a deliverable — the audited harness
  (`libs.research.axis_screen`) produces the survivor verdict, negative screens are first-class,
  every construction tried is logged (garden-of-forking-paths accounting), timestamp alignment
  declared, zero promotion authority at screen stage.

## 4. TRACK-RECORD, LEADERBOARD AND CHAMPIONSHIP MINING (MT5-native ground, first-class)

The MT5 universe's native intelligence layer, hunted permanently and broad:

- **Published track records:** MQL5 Signals, Myfxbook, FX Blue, Darwinex/DarwinIA, ZuluTrade,
  eToro, Collective2, broker follower products — thousands of verified statement-backed records.
- **Championships and contests:** trading championships and broker contests with public
  trade-by-trade archives; era archives (pre-MQL5 communities, dead-forum attachments — posted
  EA source and MT4/MT5 statements, CDX-recoverable).
- **Winner forensics (master 26) and reverse-engineering:** infer entry/exit logic, session bias,
  instrument set, sizing and risk habits from published statements; every reverse-engineered
  mechanism becomes a preregistered hypothesis in the desk's own families, never a copied signal.
- **Failure intelligence (master 25):** blown accounts, dead EAs, teardown/refutation genres
  (RU `поделка`, EN teardown forums) — negative knowledge at zero cost; the graveyard grows from
  OTHER people's capital.
- **Copy-flow and reverse copy-trade:** the RU `автоследование` layer, the CN `反向跟单` vendor
  genre, follower-flow structure — mined as MECHANISM (how retail flow is generated, routed and
  faded) with the standing debias prior: retail ruin is largely COST EXTRACTION, not directional
  wrongness — "fade retail" is a graveyarded folk premise; any retail-sentiment-contrarian screen
  carries that prior.
- **Selection-bias defense for public winners (master 23), mandatory:** leaderboards are
  maximally survivorship-biased and self-selected; every leaderboard-derived hypothesis is
  screened through the unchanged gates with explicit survivorship modelling. The
  elite-performer/leaderboard registry (master 24) records who, where, what claim, what
  verification state — claims typed under the claim-type/external-evidence law (master 22).
- **External strategy engines are workers, never validators; the X creator graph is seeds, never
  a universe** (elite mandate Part II). Better-system assimilation is universal: anything
  measurably better than ours is studied, its mechanism extracted, ours upgraded.

## 5. EVIDENCE AND CONVERSION (mining is not the product — conversion is)

- **§33 MINED-TO-WIRED, binding over any conflicting cadence:** every carded find carries exactly
  one inline disposition by end of next cycle — `[§33: wired -> artifact]` / `screened` /
  `killed` / `deferred(DATE)`. Conversion is credited from ARTIFACTS ON DISK that postdate the
  find, never from a report. While anything owes a disposition, digs refuse to start
  (`data/mining_suspended`). Backlog worked highest-ROI first, expensive tier first; a kill is
  not a conversion; the conversion ratchet only tightens.
- **Every discovery is a structured packet** (source, claim, mechanism, evidence class, cost to
  verify, MT5 instrument it informs) — a find that updates no frontier region is a number in a
  file.
- **§35** every finding gets a gap-register row; the GAP_REGISTER is the only organ that drives
  work; re-rank at cycle start, stamped. **§36** no ungoverned artifact: every doc claimed by a
  law on arrival (`docs/research/ARTIFACT_GOVERNANCE.md`). **§37** the carry-over brief is worked
  FIRST, in order.
- **Negative knowledge is a deliverable and is REVERSIBLE** — known-barren ground is recorded,
  never permanently excluded; re-open on a named enabling change only (L1.16a).
- Before ending any cycle: **"Which artifact on disk is different because of what was mined?"**
- **THE CATCH-UP LAW (LAWS §2a) applies with full force here:** conversion, screening and wiring
  permanently chase the raw growth of discovery — the backlog is a ratcheted metric that only
  closes, and every weakness any gate or audit surfaces is attacked the same cycle, not queued
  into decay.

## 6. VALIDATION (the gauntlet; the bar is a constant for life)

- **TWO-STAGE DISCOVERY LAW (sealed direction):** the backtest gauntlet is a SCREEN with ZERO
  promotion authority — generation volume is unbounded and can never create a phantom edge there.
  Promotion happens ONLY from pre-registered forward evidence: Holm-corrected, concurrent slots
  capped at `MAX_FORWARD_SLOTS=12`, registry-tracked, admission by EV-rank, always-full-never-over
  (slot_budget_exceeded + clock-saturation pin both sides). The confirmation bar never rises with
  generation and never loosens under pressure.
- **Preregistration before the run:** direction, window, threshold as constants; kill criteria
  bind BEFORE execution; sweeping windows and reporting the best is data-mining our own collector.
- **Full trial accounting:** every target-horizon cell is a DSR-counted trial; report `t_raw`,
  `n_trials`, `E_MAX`, `t_deflated` together; variants CONSIDERED are priced, not just kept.
- **Novelty gate:** every hypothesis screened against the graveyard first
  (`libs/alpha_factory/hypothesis_novelty`) — a redundant hypothesis burns multiplicity budget
  twice. **Research memory:** every outcome, including negative, logged
  (`scripts/research_memory.py`) — a forgetting factory re-runs its own experiments.
- **Mechanism-appropriate targets:** cross-sectional relative return for selection signals,
  absolute timing return for timing signals, across mechanism-appropriate horizons — never a
  reflexive default target.
- **Event-shaped edges go through the event-study gate** (`libs/validation/event_study.py`);
  ~2 non-zero days in 30 reads as noise on every continuous statistic.
- **Validation adversary + false-negative defense, in permanent tension:** every survivor is
  attacked (adversarial validation, L1.7); AND a good alpha lost to an accidentally-unclearable
  bar costs as much as a false one — gate-optimality is monitored (accept/reject histograms; a
  gate passing ~0% or ~100% carries zero information and is a defect). Cascade testing orders
  cheap kills first; no arbitrary top-N choke point (master 42); complete search-selection
  accounting (master 44).
- **Capacity parity (L1.18a):** judged against ONE SLEEVE, never the whole book; the score goes
  flat above sufficiency; both capacity bands hunted concurrently; retirement only at
  growth_runway < 1, and then hunt one tier larger NOW.

## 7. SURVIVORS AND THE FACTORY (the renewable engine)

The survivor factory is the primary renewable engine: continuous meaningful candidate production,
quota-free and generation-first — hypothesis testing is the desk's PRIMARY output and is never
crowded out by meta work. Survivors are a state machine (master 60): immediate screening,
immediate forward shadow, no arbitrary calendar delay, minimum-capital live probes when the
ladder says so. Every survivor spawns **descendants** (parameter-neighbourhood, mechanism
transfer, regime variants); every failure feeds the failure → information → descendant loop; near
survivors enter the repair factory. Survivor independence is measured (orthogonality before
opportunity reduction); portfolio-first value — marginal E[log W] contribution, never standalone
record. **Net alpha replenishment** (master 67) is the factory's output metric: edges added minus
edges decayed, positive, permanently.

## 8. WIRING, ORPHANS AND CADENCE (III.16: unwired or idle is a defect)

- A capability is DONE when something runs it on a schedule or live path and leaves an artifact.
  The orphan queue (`scripts/audit_mt5_capability_reuse.py`: REACHABLE / UNWIRED_REVIEW /
  VENUE_SPECIFIC) is standing triage: wire positive-EV venue-agnostic organs to the MT5 desk,
  block/archive venue-specific ones explicitly, never manufacture usage. Orphan eradication is a
  master law (11); reachability is transitive and lazy imports hide it — walk the import graph.
- **Order of operations, every cycle:** STEP 0 integrity watch (money leaks outrank everything);
  STEP 1 mechanical sweeps (max_audit, findings report, audit coverage); STEP 2 GENERATION (owed
  generate runs + clock saturation BEFORE meta work); STEP 3 triage/build; STEP 4
  self-interrogation + blind-spot logging + cycle report. Budget exhausted mid-sequence leaves
  NAMED debts, never silent skips.
- **Ratchets everywhere (L1.0/L2.0):** coverage, breadth, conversion, uptime, spans — floored on
  first measurement, staleness-fenced, self-initiated toward 100%.
- **Anti-rubber-stamp:** every verification cites path AND value on the same line; the graded
  floor is 5 citations per cycle; bare "verified/fine" is rejected. **Blind-spot origin:** every
  gap logged tagged self/guard/principal — principal-found is the cycle's top defect.
  **Findings lifecycle:** accepted findings unfixed past 14 days are defects, named in the
  report. **No orphaned recommendation:** every recommendation reaches a disposition; undisposed
  past 24h is a defect.
- **The midnight adversarial battery (master 187–207)** runs as written: claim falsification,
  fake-productivity attack, hidden/unwired component attack, stale-score attack,
  economic-leakage attack, survivor adversarial attack, no protection for previous work, audit
  terminates in ACTION, the audit itself is falsifiable.

## 9. REPORTING AND HONESTY

Structured probabilistic outputs; uncertainty decomposed; disagreement is uncertainty, resolved
by targeted search, never by voting. UNMEASURED is a real answer and is said out loud. No metric
gaming, no fake ceilings, no fake completion, no silent failure. Every cycle report scores BOTH
supreme readings — what it did for growth, what it did for the discovery rate — and names the
single highest-EV improvement to compounding available right now. The desk finds its own gaps so
the principal never has to; the recursion rule turns every new probe angle that finds a gap into
a permanent automated check. Fewer, sharper, evidence-backed pushes beat a long list — and the
push never stops.
