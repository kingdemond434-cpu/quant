CAPACITY SIMULATOR (scripts/capacity_simulator.py)
  Principal asked "how much at Gate-0". That is computable from venue filters, not estimable, so
  this replaces my rule-of-thumb with Binance's real MIN_NOTIONAL + LOT_SIZE step per symbol,
  combined with the desk's MEASURED L2 cost model.
  KEY INSIGHT: slippage does NOT bind at small size -- the cost model shows impact RISING with
  size (AAVE 0.51bps @ $100 -> 1.97bps @ $2500), so small orders are cheaper. The floor is LOT
  ROUNDING, and the real hazard of breaching it is LEG MISMATCH: if the spot and perp legs round
  differently the position stops being delta-neutral, i.e. it silently becomes a directional bet
  the strategy never intended.

  RAW OUTPUT: ~$15/carry, ~$150 for 10 carries. I DO NOT TRUST THAT NUMBER -- two defects:
  (1) futures min_notional reads 0.0 for every symbol (filter-key mismatch in my code; Binance
      futures enforces $5-20), so the floor is UNDERSTATED. Sizing must not use it until fixed.
  (2) COOKIEUSDT shows 59.17bps slippage FLAT across every bucket ($100..$2500). Flat impact
      across a 25x size range is not physically plausible -- either a degenerate cost-model entry
      or a genuinely illiquid symbol. If real: 4 legs ~ 2.4% round-trip against ~0.7%/mo funding
      harvest = the carry is structurally unprofitable on that symbol at ANY size.

  ARGUABLY THE BIGGER FINDING: the live paper universe is micro-cap alts (COOKIE, 1000CAT, MOVE,
  TST). Their liquidity may not support this strategy at any capital level. That is a universe
  problem, not a sizing problem, and it would explain part of the -0.98% paper drift.
