CREATIVE HYPOTHESIS THAT ACTUALLY PAID: instead of hunting another alpha (25 tested today, 1
worked), optimise the one that WORKS. Funding persistence is real (IC +0.43, top-decile +29%/yr).
_MIN_HOLD_H=24 was set to STOP CHURN (gap #42), never optimised against measured data. Both sides
of the trade-off are now measured -- persistence decay AND per-symbol round-trip cost -- so the
optimum is computable rather than guessable.

THE 168h "OPTIMUM" IS NOISE, stated plainly so nobody acts on it: at k=21 periods there are only
~7 non-overlapping rotations in the sample. Its gross (35.14%) breaks the range every other row
occupies (23-26%), and 120h's 17.78% is the same instability inverted. n=7 is not a result.

WHAT IS SOLID: gross capture is roughly FLAT (~24%) from 16h to 72h while cost falls monotonically
with hold length. Net therefore improves for ONE reliable reason -- COST AMORTISATION, not better
selection. Defensible landing zone: 48-72h (n=17-22 rotations, adequately sampled), worth ~+11pp/yr
versus the current 24h. Longer holds are also strictly SAFER operationally: less trading, less
churn, fewer opportunities to be wrong.

INDEPENDENT CONFIRMATION OF THE COST STORY: at an 8h hold the book would pay 62.6%/yr in costs
against 23.4%/yr gross. Churn was always the dominant term -- this reproduces the hurdle-report
finding (7.75x cost/funding) from a completely different direction, using different data.
