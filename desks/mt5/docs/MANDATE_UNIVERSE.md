- Hunt **scalp, intraday and swing** mechanisms across the **whole MT5 universe** (every symbol
  the broker exposes; discover dynamically — symbols, metadata, ticks, spreads, contract sizes,
  stops/freeze levels, sessions). MT5 is execution truth; it is never the whole information
  universe.
- **Research everything. Trade only what earns authority.** A mechanism earns authority through:
  deterministic gates (t>2, n>60, PF>1.05, maxDD>-30R), untouched chronological WF OOS (all
  folds positive), realistic costs incl. 2x stress, then shadow-forward proof (≥50 trades or
  14 days), then portfolio-level incremental contribution.
- No per-symbol optimization; fixed deployed params per mechanism family. Hypothesis IDs,
  parameter/window/instrument records, permanent searchable graveyard for failures.
- **MICRO_ALPHA_LAB (scalp factory)**: M5/intraday scalp families run as a separate research
  branch with brutal cost gates (net edge after spread+commission+slippage, 2x stress, WF OOS).
  Never alter the 4 validated gold sleeves.

## Supreme rule
> Stop making the machine broader unless a new component has a clear economic purpose.
> Make every existing layer deeper, more causal, more point-in-time, cheaper, more profitable.
> The main limitation is finding persistent edge before everyone else does.

> combining. A free dataset that consumes engineer-hours and produces zero unique information is
