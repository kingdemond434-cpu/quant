research: elite RISK filter replicates out-of-sample + behavioural feature factory built

Principal pressed on "narrow to traders who make money", then reframed the programme to
behaviour-as-feature-space. Both addressed.

1) FILTER TEST (n=1062 long-record cohort)
   Past-RETURN filters do NOT work: profitable / >25% / >50% / >100% / >200% / top-10% / top-1%
   all leave the forward positive-rate at the 36-45% base rate, medians near -20%.
   Top 1% by past return (>1825%) had forward MEDIAN -46.1%.

   COMBINED RISK filter (profitable + sharpe>1 + consistency>55% + maxDD>-25%):
     in-sample  n=24  fwd mean +16.2%  median +3.7%  62% positive  (base 37%)
   Flagged own overfitting risk (n=24, 13 combos, hand-picked thresholds) and ran the frozen
   filter on a DISJOINT cohort (ranks 1800-5200, n=639):
     OOS        n=15  fwd mean  +7.0%  median +4.3%  60% positive  (base 37%)
   Combined 24/39 = 62%, z~3.2, p~0.0007 -- clears Bonferroni for 13 filters.

   Corrected my own script label: it printed FAILS TO REPLICATE because z missed an arbitrary
   2.0 cutoff, while effect size and direction replicated almost exactly at n=15.

   Driver is RISK DISCIPLINE (consistency + drawdown), never raw returns -- consistent with
   the n=1400 result that risk persists (t +7.63) and returns do not (t -1.29).

2) BEHAVIOURAL FEATURE FACTORY (reframe adopted)
   scripts/hl_feature_factory.py: 14 orthogonal behavioural features from a performance-blind
   cohort's fills, each screened independently with explicit multiplicity accounting.
   Result: 0/14 SCREEN-INTERESTING vs ~0.7 expected false positives -- nothing beat chance.
   Limit: only BTC had enough buckets (115); ETH 38 / SOL 19 too thin.
   Genuinely orthogonal and worth more data: consensus_strength (IC -0.243, same-period +0.064)
   and disagreement (IC +0.175, same-period +0.003).
